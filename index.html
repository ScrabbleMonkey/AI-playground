UK Defence Investment Accelerates with Historic Spending Commitment

The government's Strategic Defence Review 2025 sets out a sustained increase in defence spending, with investment focused on long-range weapons, cyber resilience and modernisation of existing capabilities. Berkshire firms BAE Systems, Thales and the Atomic Weapons Establishment are expected to be major beneficiaries.
